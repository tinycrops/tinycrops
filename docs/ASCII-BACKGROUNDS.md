# Construct the environment alongside the ASCII subject

Background construction is part of the studio artwork, with its own spatial structure and material. Dithering only the subject or adding uniform noise does not fulfill it. Provide an intelligible setting by default, while retaining isolated source controls and honoring requests for deliberate emptiness.

This first implementation constructs distant ridges, a horizon, perspective floor marks and approximate contact darkness. A seeded irregular threshold screen supplies monochrome grain independently of the subject glyphs. Keep foreground contrast stronger and readable text areas quieter. No blur.

The scenery is an authored illustrative setting, not a physical simulation. Glyph splats do not provide complete solid-surface occlusion; the avatar's original dithering algorithm is unknown. Visual review remains necessary. Technical checks do not establish audience comprehension.

The preceding object-only and colour artwork remain in Git history. Source glyph IDs and geometric mappings remain retained locally with the deterministic generator. Asset hashes and render provenance are in assets/studio/provenance.json.
